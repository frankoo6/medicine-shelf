Keep your list safe

How to save and recover your list
  1. Tap Save a backup and keep the file in a private location you can reach if this phone is lost.
  2. Check that the file is there, then choose Yes, I kept it below.
  3. To recover your list, open Tools → Restore a backup and choose that file. Review the date and people before confirming replacement.

The file is not encrypted and includes medicine details. Anyone who can open it can read them. Your list does not sync between phones.

    Using the list on two phones

    Medicine Shelf has no account, so two phones don't share a list on their own. To copy your list to someone else's phone:

    1. Tap Copy my list to another phone below and send the file to them (text, email or AirDrop).
    2. On their phone, open Medicine Shelf and tap Restore a backup, then pick that file.

    This replaces whatever list is on their phone. After that, changes made on one phone don't reach the other, so decide who keeps the list up to date and copy it again after a refill or recount.

    How the dates are worked out

    The count goes down by the daily amount from the day you last counted, so you only need to update it when you pick up a refill or recount.

    Order on is the day you'd run out, minus the days your pharmacy needs and a few extra days to be safe. It is never earlier than the day insurance is likely to allow the refill. That day is an estimate based on when you last picked it up, unless you enter the date the pharmacy gave you.

    Medicines that need a new prescription each fill (or have no refills left) show Contact prescriber a week before the order date, so the office has time to send it.

    I requested a refill and It’s ready for pickup record where a refill stands, so you can see whether you already called it in. The count only goes up when you tap Picked up a refill, and the card stays urgent if the supply is about to run out.

    Medicines taken only as needed don't run on a schedule, so they show as "order now" when the count is at or below the number you choose.

    Estimated days left and Estimated left are estimates: they assume every dose is taken as scheduled. Each card shows when it was last counted by hand, and after 90 days (you can change this under Timing) it asks for a quick recount.

    Privacy and limits

    Your list is stored only in this browser on this device. Medicine Shelf does not send it to any server, your doctor or the people who host this page.

    Anything you export leaves the app: backup files, calendar entries and printed lists can show your medicines to whoever sees them. Keep them somewhere private. If others can see your calendar, turn on Keep calendar reminders private: reminders then just say "Check medicine supply".

    Because it lives on this device, it does not sync between phones, and clearing your browser data erases it. Save a backup now and then.
