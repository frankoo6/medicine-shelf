How to set up Medicine Shelf

Medicine Shelf helps you keep track of how much medicine you have at home, and when to reorder.

It is free. You do not need an account.

The app link:

https://frankoo6.github.io/medicine-shelf/

Take your time. Do one step, then the next. A friend or family member can help.

Part 1: Open the app

If you have the flyer:

  1. Open the camera on your phone.
  2. Point it at the square code on the flyer.
  3. Tap the link that pops up.

If you do not have the flyer:

  1. Open the web browser. On iPhone, use Safari. On Android, use Chrome.
  2. Type the link from the box above into the address bar at the top.
  3. Tap Go or the Enter key.

You will see the words “Stay ahead of medicine refills”.

Part 2: Put it on your home screen

This makes it open like an app. It also works without internet. It also keeps your list safer.

On iPhone (use Safari):

  1. Tap the Share button. It is a square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom or top of the screen.
  2. Scroll down the list.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add.
  5. Look for the Medicine Shelf icon on your home screen. From now on, open it from there.

On iPhone, this step is important. Safari can erase the list if you skip it.

On Android (use Chrome):

  1. You may see a message: “Add Medicine Shelf to your home screen”. If so, tap Install, then skip to step 5.
  2. If not, tap the menu. It is three dots in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Tap Install or Add.
  5. Look for the Med Shelf icon on your home screen. From now on, open it from there.

Want to do this later? Tap Not now.

Part 3: Add the first person

  1. Open Medicine Shelf.
  2. Find the box that says “Who takes medicine in your home? Start with one person.”
  3. Type a name, for example “Mom”, or your first name.
  4. Tap Start my list.

Want to look around first? Tap See an example first. When you are ready, tap Clear the example and start my own.

Words too small? Tap Make the text larger.

To add another person later, tap Add a person, type the name, and tap Save.

Part 4: Add the first medicine

Have the medicine bottle or box next to you.

  1. Tap Add a medicine.
  2. Under For, pick the person.
  3. Under Medicine name, type the name as it is on the label.
  4. Under Counted as, pick tablets, capsules, mL, patches, inhalers or doses.
  5. Under How many a day, type the number from the label or from your pharmacist.
  6. Count the medicine you have.
  7. Under How many you have right now, type that number.
  8. Check Date counted. It should be today.
  9. Tap Save.

The other boxes are optional. You can fill them in later.

Made a mistake? Tap Cancel and start again.

This app only counts your supply. It does not give medical advice. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about how to take your medicine.

Part 5: Your list stays on your phone

  • Your list is never sent anywhere.
  • Nobody else can see it. Not the doctor’s office. Not us.
  • If you lose your phone, the list is lost too.

So save a backup file:

  1. After you add your first medicine, the app asks you to back up. Tap Save a backup.
  2. Keep the file somewhere safe, like your email, the Files app, or a cloud drive.
  3. The app asks “Did you keep the backup file?” Tap Yes, I kept it.

The app will remind you to save a new backup from time to time.

You can also find Save a backup under Tools, near the bottom of the app.

New phone? Open the app on the new phone. Tap Restore a backup, then pick your backup file.

Need help?

Ask a family member, a friend, or the office that gave you this guide.
