RxMedicine Shelf

Know when to reorder your medicines, before you run out

A free app that counts down each medicine and tells you the day to call the pharmacy.

  1. Scan with your phone camera.Point the camera at the code and tap the link.
  2. Add it to your home screen.iPhone: tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen". Android: tap Install.
  3. Add each medicine.Enter how many you have and how many you take a day. The app does the rest.
